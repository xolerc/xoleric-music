Features

🎵

Background Playback

Full MediaSession integration with notification controls, lock screen support, and seamless background playback.

🌐

Dark Glassmorphism UI

Stunning dark theme with glassmorphism cards and customizable neon accent colors.

📋

Smart Playlists

Recently Played, Most Played, Favorites, and Never Played playlists auto-generated from your listening habits.

🔍

Powerful Search

Search across songs, artists, albums, and genres with instant results.

💫

Multi-Queue

Manage your queue with add to queue, play next, and drag-to-reorder support.

⏱

Sleep Timer

Set a sleep timer by minutes or until end of current song. Perfect for bedtime listening.
