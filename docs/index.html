Music Player & Download Manager

Your music stays yours. No ads. No tracking. Pure audio experience with built-in download manager.

Features

🎵

Background Playback

Full audio playback with just_audio engine, notification controls, and lock screen support.

🌐

Dark Glassmorphism UI

Material 3 dark theme with glassmorphism cards and customizable neon accent colors.

📥

Download Manager

Built-in download engine with pause, resume, cancel. Download music, videos, and images from URLs.

🔍

Smart Search

Instant search across songs, artists, albums with real-time filtering.

📋

Playlists

Create, manage, and organize custom playlists. Persistent storage across sessions.

💫

Queue Management

Drag-to-reorder queue, play next, add to queue with full control.

❤

Favorites

Quick-access favorites with persistent storage.

🎶

Lyrics

Dedicated lyrics screen for your songs.
